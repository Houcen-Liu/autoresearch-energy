# Citing and reusing

If you reuse the harness, please cite the underlying work it builds on:

- Karpathy, A. (2026). *autoresearch*. https://github.com/karpathy/autoresearch (MIT)
- Karsten, M., Dragomir, A. C., Apsan, R., Stoico, V., Malavolta, I. (2025).
  *Experiment Runner: A framework for automated measurement-based experiments*.
  Science of Computer Programming.
- Sallou, J., et al. (2023). *EnergiBridge: Empowering Software Sustainability through
  Cross-Platform Energy Measurement*.

`experiment-runner/` is vendored here so the exact version used is pinned with the
results; it remains the property of its authors.
