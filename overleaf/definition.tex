\section{Experiment Definition}\label{sec:definition}

Following the Goal--Question--Metric template \cite{wohlin12}, the goal of this experiment is:

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Analyze} self-hosted LLM proposer architectures and agent-loop configurations\\
\textbf{for the purpose of} evaluation\\
\textbf{with respect to} their energy efficiency and task accuracy\\
\textbf{from the point of view of} researchers and practitioners who want to self-host LLMs\\
\textbf{in the context of} the \texttt{autoresearch} agentic AutoML loop running on self-hosted dual-GPU hardware.
\end{quote}

The goal refines into two research questions:

\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.2em]
\item[RQ1.] \emph{How do loop configurations affect energy and accuracy?}
\item[RQ2.] \emph{Which configurations sit on the energy/accuracy Pareto frontier?}
\end{description}

RQ1 is analytic: it quantifies the main and two-way interaction effects of proposer architecture, patience, and loop budget on GPU-board session energy, and reports the corresponding accuracy model, device-associated energy decomposition, and GPU energy attributable to non-retained iterations. RQ2 is synthetic: it aggregates the same measurements into a sample Pareto frontier over (GPU-board session energy, final accuracy), including the designated baseline cell. A CPU-served proposer re-evaluation was planned but is not included in this report while its scope awaits supervisor feedback.

Figure~\ref{fig:gqm} maps the goal to questions and metrics. GPU-board energy is measured through NVML, and AMD CPU-package energy is reconstructed from the raw EnergiBridge counter as a broader-scope sensitivity analysis; neither scope is a wall-power or full-system measurement. Accuracy metrics are ground-truth workload outcomes (validation accuracy during the loop and CIFAR-10 test-set accuracy at session end), deliberately distinct from the agent's own keep/revert judgments, which this experiment treats as behavioral signals rather than accuracy measures.
